Update the yard without losing its character

Make room for the way
you live outdoors.

On residential streets near Montford Drive and Park Road, lots vary in age, size, sun, and slope. Some have established shade trees and foundation planting beds; others have more open lawn or space to gather outdoors. Choose updates that work with the existing site rather than assuming every yard needs the same treatment.

Services that can help

  • Lawn maintenance for mowing, aeration, fertilizer application, and weed control.
  • Planting and bed maintenance when plantings have outgrown the space or need refreshing.
  • Mulch and pine needle installation for a clean, defined bed.
  • Patio and retaining wall projects for a better-defined outdoor area or grade change.

Consider shade, slope, and the rules

Before planting, note which beds receive morning or afternoon sun and where water drains. If a yard slopes, identify whether soil washes away or water stands after rain. A retaining wall should not be treated as a quick fix for drainage without assessing the source and destination of the water.

For patios, walls, or a major landscape update, check with your HOA if one applies and ask whether permits or utility marking are needed. Share access constraints, stairs, gates, and project dimensions when requesting an estimate.

Montford landscaping FAQs

Can you help plan a patio in a small backyard?+

Patio design and construction are listed SLS services. Share approximate dimensions, access, slope, and intended use so the team can discuss whether the project is a fit.

What if there is standing water in the yard?+

Tell us where and when water collects. Drainage depends on the site and may require assessment by a drainage professional before landscape work is planned.

Can I ask for both lawn and planting-bed work?+

Yes. List each requested service in your estimate form and we'll confirm scope and availability.
