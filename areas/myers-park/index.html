A yard shaped by its setting

Care for established trees,
shade, and garden beds.

From the tree-lined streets near Queens Road West to homes around the Little Sugar Creek Greenway, Myers Park yards often include mature canopy, shade, and established beds. Leaves can arrive in waves and compete with lawn maintenance. A useful plan starts by noticing which parts of the property are sunny, how water moves after a storm, and what you want to keep.

Services Myers Park homeowners ask about

  • Lawn care and maintenance for the sunlit areas that need regular attention.
  • Leaf removal when fall coverage outpaces your own cleanup routine.
  • Planting and bed maintenance to refresh existing beds or talk through plants suited to the actual light.
  • Mulch or pine needle installation for beds that need a fresh, tidy finish.

Start with the conditions on your property

Shade under a mature canopy differs from a bed receiving afternoon sun. Before changing a planting area, note where sunlight reaches it, what has struggled, and whether the ground stays wet or dries quickly. Keep new mulch away from trunks and plant stems, and avoid piling material over existing roots.

For a patio or retaining wall, mention grade changes, drainage, access, and any neighborhood requirements. SLS will discuss the requested scope and current availability; major drainage or structural concerns may call for a qualified specialist.

What happens next

  1. Send the street or neighborhood and a few details about the yard.
  2. Talk through the service you need and any site conditions.
  3. Confirm scope, estimate, and timing directly with the team.
