Plan for the yard you have

One neighborhood.
Different property needs.

SouthPark homes range from established streets near the SouthPark Mall corridor to recently renovated properties with new planting beds. One yard may have an open front lawn while the next is shaped by mature shade or a narrow side passage. Sun exposure, slope, and access all affect the right maintenance approach; describe your property instead of relying on a standard package.

Services for SouthPark properties

  • Lawn care and maintenance for mowing, aeration, fertilizer application, and weed control.
  • Planting and bed maintenance for refreshed entries and beds with changing light conditions.
  • Leaf removal to clear seasonal buildup from lawn and garden areas.
  • Mulch and pine needle installation for a clean finish to the beds.

Details that help the estimate

Note which areas are in sun or shade, what is growing well, where water collects, and whether crews can reach the back yard through a gate. For a patio or retaining wall, include rough dimensions and any grade changes. If your property is in an HOA, check whether there are design or contractor rules before the work is scheduled.

Where drainage is a concern, tell us where the water comes from and where it goes. A wall or new planting bed should not redirect water onto a neighbor's property; significant drainage issues may need a drainage professional.

A simple start

  1. Share the property address and the project areas.
  2. Talk through the goal, site conditions, and available services.
  3. Confirm estimate, schedule, and scope with the SLS team.
