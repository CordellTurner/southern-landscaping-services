Care for a changing landscape

From town-center lots
to tree-lined yards.

Around downtown Matthews and Stumptown Park, homes sit among a mix of sunny lawns, mature trees, and established beds. Farther out, some properties have more wooded edges. Leaf fall, root competition, and patchy shade can make routine care less straightforward. Recommendations should start with the property, not a one-size plan.

Landscaping services in Matthews

  • Lawn care and maintenance for mowing, aeration, fertilization, and weed control.
  • Leaf removal for seasonal buildup in lawn and bed areas.
  • Planting and garden bed care for beds that need a refresh.
  • Mulch and pine needle installation to renew existing planting beds.

What to include with your request

Tell us which part of Matthews the property is in, how much sun the lawn and beds receive, and what work you want done. If the yard has a slope, soft spots, or standing water, mention that before planning new planting or hardscaping. For HOA properties, check approval rules for plantings, walls, and materials.

If a project involves a new patio or retaining wall, flag drainage and access issues early. Where water is entering a home or affecting neighboring property, consult a qualified drainage professional before construction.
