Careful choices for
close-in Charlotte lots.

Near Latta Park and East Boulevard, many Dilworth homes sit on compact lots with sidewalks, mature trees, and outdoor areas that serve more than one purpose. Tree shade, roots, and narrow passages can affect plant growth and project access. Start by identifying the spaces you use most and the areas that are frustrating to maintain.

Useful services for Dilworth properties

  • Planting and bed care for a simpler, more intentional front entry or side-yard bed.
  • Leaf removal for lawns and planting areas under mature trees.
  • Mulch and pine needles to refresh beds without expanding their footprint.
  • Patio and retaining wall projects to discuss outdoor living and changes in grade.

Plan around access and approvals

Before requesting work, note gate width, steps, parking, and where materials can be brought into the yard. For a wall or patio, mention slope, drainage, and nearby utilities. Some Dilworth properties may be subject to historic-district review or neighborhood rules; homeowners should verify approval requirements with the City and their association before construction.

How to get a useful estimate

  1. Send your address or nearest cross street and describe the area.
  2. Tell us what you want changed and what you want to preserve.
  3. Review the scope and schedule with SLS before work begins.
