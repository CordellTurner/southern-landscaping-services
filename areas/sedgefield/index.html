Think about the whole yard before adding work

Look at where runoff travels, where mature roots extend, and how crews can reach the work area. Protect tree trunks when adding mulch and avoid covering roots with deep layers. If grass thins in a shady area, note how many hours of direct sun it gets rather than assuming more fertilizer is needed.

For a new patio or retaining wall, provide rough dimensions and describe any slope or drainage issues. The city, HOA, or historic-district requirements may apply based on the property and scope, so homeowners should check those details before construction.
