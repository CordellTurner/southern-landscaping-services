What landscaping services does SLS offer?+

We handle lawn care and maintenance, aeration and fertilization, weed control, mulch and pine needle installation, leaf cleanup, planting and bed care, and patio or retaining wall projects. See the service pages.

Where does Southern Landscaping Services work?+

We’re based in South Charlotte and serve homeowners around the area. If you’re not sure whether we cover your neighborhood, just reach out and we’ll let you know. Ask about your location.

How do I request an estimate?+

Call or text (410) 961-1473, email alex@southernlandscapingserv.com, or use the estimate form on the contact page. We’re happy to talk through your project and give you a better idea of next steps.

Can I text to ask for an estimate?+

Yes. You can text us at (410) 961-1473 to start a conversation about your property, or use the SMS link on the contact page.

Does SLS offer commercial, HOA, or contractor services?+

Our focus is residential landscaping, but if you have a commercial property, HOA, or contractor question, we’d still be glad to hear from you and talk through what’s possible.

What should I include in my estimate request?+

Include your contact info, the type of property, the service you’re interested in, and a quick description of the work. If you’re checking availability, add your location too.

Are the photos on this website completed SLS projects?+

No. The photos on our inspiration page are illustrative; they are not verified Southern Landscaping Services projects or before-and-after results.

Does the website list a street address or office hours?+

We don't list a walk-in office address or set business hours online. Call, text, or email us and we'll get back to you about your project.
