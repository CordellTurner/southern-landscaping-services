What landscaping services does SLS offer?+

The public service list includes lawn care and maintenance; aeration and fertilizer application; weed control; mulch and pine needle sales and installation; leaf removal; planting and bed maintenance; and patio and retaining wall design and construction. See the service pages.

Where does Southern Landscaping Services work?+

The company identifies South Charlotte as its service area. A more detailed neighborhood list or exact service boundary is not published. Ask about your location.

How do I request an estimate?+

Call or text (410) 961-1473, email alex@southernlandscapingserv.com, or send the estimate form on the contact page. The public company website invites customers to request a free estimate.

Can I text to ask for an estimate?+

Yes. The public company website says to text YARD to 410-961-1473 for a free estimate. You can also use the SMS link on the contact page.

Does SLS offer commercial, HOA, or contractor services?+

The public website focuses on residential landscaping and does not publish specific eligibility or service details for commercial properties, HOAs, or general contractors. Contact the team to confirm whether it can help with your property.

What should I include in my estimate request?+

Share your contact information, property type, the service you’re interested in, and a short description of the work. If you have a question about service availability, include your property's location.

Are the photos on this website completed SLS projects?+

The photos currently available from the company website are not identified as customer projects. On this site, they are labeled as illustrative images and are not presented as a verified portfolio.

Does the website list a street address or office hours?+

A street address and office hours are not listed in the public company information used for this website. You can reach the team by phone or email.
