A little about us

Neighbors first.
Landscapers always.

Southern Landscaping Services is a family-owned business proudly serving the South Charlotte area for over 30 years. Alex Lieske moved to Charlotte with his family and took the reins with a simple goal: make SLS the premier residential landscaping service in the area.

Today, Alex, Rob, and the team bring care, experience, and a personal touch to every yard. We listen to what you want, offer honest guidance, and take pride in work that feels right at home.
