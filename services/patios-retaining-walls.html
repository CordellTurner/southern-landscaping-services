Patios and retaining walls in Charlotte

Make a tricky slope
or empty space useful.

A patio or retaining wall is a lasting change to your property. Start with how you want to use the space, then check grades, drainage, access, and any neighborhood approval requirements.

COMMON PROBLEM

A slope you cannot use

Uneven ground can limit where you sit, walk, or gather. A retaining wall may be part of a solution, but grading and water movement need careful consideration.

SIGNS TO PLAN A PROJECT

Cracked or leaning walls

Movement, bulging, or poor drainage around an existing wall deserves an assessment before repairs or added landscaping.

THE BENEFIT

A defined place outdoors

A thoughtfully planned patio can make outdoor seating easier; a well-planned wall can help organize a change in grade.

01

Share your goals and site conditions

Tell us how you want to use the space and what you know about access, slope, drainage, utilities, and existing walls.

02

Discuss the project scope

Patios and retaining walls are listed SLS services. The team can talk through your request, site needs, and whether the project is a fit.

03

Confirm requirements before work

Review the proposed scope, estimate, and any permits, utility locates, engineering, or HOA approvals that may apply to the property.

Can a retaining wall solve a wet-yard problem?+

Not by itself in every case. Water sources, grading, soil, and discharge location matter. Describe the drainage issue and ask whether the work needs a drainage professional or engineer.

Do I need approval before building a patio or wall?+

Requirements depend on the property, project, and neighborhood. Check with the City of Charlotte or Mecklenburg County, your HOA, and any applicable historic district before construction.

Can I get an estimate from a photo?+

Photos and rough dimensions help start the conversation, but the team may need more information or a site visit before confirming scope and pricing.
