Mulch and pine straw in South Charlotte

Refresh the beds.
Protect the plants.

A fresh layer can make planting beds look finished and help moderate soil moisture. The right material and depth depend on the bed and what is already there.

A COMMON PROBLEM

Faded or patchy beds

Old mulch, exposed soil, and weeds can make an otherwise cared-for yard look unfinished.

SIGNS TO REFRESH

Soil showing through

Consider a refresh when the bed covering has broken down or thinned enough that weeds and bare soil are becoming a nuisance.

CHOOSE WITH CARE

Keep stems and trunks clear

Mulch should not be piled against plant stems or tree trunks. If an old layer is already deep, ask before adding more.

Should I choose mulch or pine needles?+

Both are commonly used in Carolina planting beds. The look you prefer, existing plants, and the layout of the bed can help guide the choice. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss available options.

How often should landscaping mulch be replaced?+

There is no one schedule for every bed. Check whether the existing layer has broken down, thinned, or become too deep before adding more. A light refresh may be all that is needed.

Can you refresh just part of my property?+

Describe the beds you want done in your estimate request so we can discuss the requested scope and availability.
