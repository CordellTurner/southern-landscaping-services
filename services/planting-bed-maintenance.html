Planting and bed care in Charlotte

Make the beds feel
like part of the home.

Planting choices work best when they fit the bed's sunlight, mature tree roots, drainage, and the upkeep you want.

COMMON CHALLENGE

Plants struggling in shade

Established trees and buildings can leave beds with very different light from one side of the house to the other.

SIGNS A BED NEEDS CARE

Overgrown or uneven planting

Declining plants, bare gaps, and edges disappearing into the lawn are good reasons to take another look at the bed.

BENEFITS

A planting plan that fits

Choosing plants for actual light and site conditions can make the garden easier to maintain and more cohesive over time.

Which plants work best in a shady Charlotte yard?+

It depends on how much direct sun the bed receives, its drainage, and nearby tree roots. Note those conditions in your request so the team can discuss suitable options. NC State's Plant Toolbox lets you look up plants for site conditions.

Can you update an existing garden bed instead of starting over?+

Yes, you can ask about refreshing the planting or caring for the existing beds. Describe what you want to keep and what you would like to change.

Can I combine planting with mulch installation?+

Let us know if the same beds need planting and a fresh covering. We can discuss the requested scope and schedule.
