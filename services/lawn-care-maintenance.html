Make sense of your lawn

Good lawn care starts
with the right question.

Charlotte lawns can behave differently in shade, heat, and compacted soil. Share what you see rather than guessing at a treatment or schedule.

WHEN TO ASK

Thin or bare patches

Worn areas, thinning grass, or soil that feels hard can point to several causes. A closer look helps determine whether aeration, routine maintenance, or another approach makes sense.

WHY IT HELPS

Care that fits the turf

Matching maintenance to the grass, season, shade, and use of the yard helps avoid doing the wrong work at the wrong time.

When should a Charlotte lawn be aerated?+

It depends on the grass type. Cool-season tall fescue is typically aerated in fall; warm-season grasses are generally aerated while actively growing in late spring or early summer. Tell us what grass you have if you know it.

Do you offer one-time lawn visits?+

Ask us about your project and current availability. We'll confirm whether the work is a fit and discuss the schedule directly.

Can you tell me what is causing a brown patch?+

Brown areas can have more than one cause, including shade, drought, disease, pests, or foot traffic. Share what you have noticed; a diagnosis may require an on-site assessment or a turf specialist.
