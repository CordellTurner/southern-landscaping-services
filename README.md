# Southern Landscaping Services

A responsive, multi-page website built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Run locally

Serve this folder from its root with any static file server and open `index.html`. The canonical URLs and sitemap assume deployment at the root of `www.southernlandscapingserv.com`. The estimate forms open a prefilled email to `alex@southernlandscapingserv.com`; they do not submit to a website backend.

## Business information and media

Company details and customer quotes are based on the public Southern Landscaping Services website at <https://www.southernlandscapingserv.com/>. The public site did not expose a standalone logo or street address, so the header uses a text wordmark with a simple, newly drawn leaf mark and no address is shown.

## Pages

- Home (`index.html`)
- Services and service detail pages (`services/`)
- About, process, core values, and team (`about/`)
- Outdoor inspiration (`projects/`)
- Service area, contact, careers, and FAQs
- Nine neighborhood landing pages (`areas/`): Myers Park, Dilworth, SouthPark, Ballantyne, Matthews, Mint Hill, Freedom Park, Sedgefield, and Montford
- Landscaping tips hub and eight homeowner guides (`resources/`)

`sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are included for search-engine discovery. The sitemap uses the company's public website domain; update it if the deployment domain changes.

## Content limitations

- The project brief names team members Alex, Rob, Ricky, and Dallas. Public company information provides background about Alex and mentions Rob; individual roles and biographies for the rest of the team were not available. Team cards disclose those limits and do not invent profiles or use stock people as portraits.
- The company's available photographs do not identify verified customer projects. The projects page presents them only as illustrative images, not as an SLS portfolio or before-and-after proof. Add project-specific pages only after the company supplies approved photos and confirmed details.
- The new neighborhood pages target the locations in the project brief and provide local homeowner guidance. They do not guarantee that every address is covered; customers are asked to confirm service availability. South Charlotte is the company's publicly stated service area, and no fixed coverage boundary, street address, or office hours are published.
- The public source focuses on residential landscaping and does not confirm commercial, HOA, or general-contractor service eligibility.
- Current job listings, hiring requirements, and a careers application process were not publicly available. The careers page directs interested people to ask the company; it does not claim a vacancy is open.
- Estimate-request forms collect a neighborhood or ZIP code and launch a prefilled email to the published company address. They do not send or store information on a server; a visitor must review and send the email. Call and SMS links are available as alternatives.
- The landscaping guides are general information, not property-specific horticultural or drainage advice. Lawn timing and plant selection links point readers to NC State Extension resources for current, site-appropriate guidance.
